## Permitted use

You may:

- accurately identify the software as derived from or based on the original WebGuard project;
- preserve and display the included official branding when installing, using, or demonstrating an
  unmodified official distribution; and
- make ordinary truthful references to Bashar Asaad as the original author and publisher.

These permissions do not imply sponsorship, endorsement, partnership, or official status for a
third-party distribution, hosted service, consultancy, or other offering.

## Official releases

Official WebGuard releases are identified through the repository and release channel designated
by Bashar Asaad as the original publisher. No repository URL or hosting account is designated in
this policy while the official public repository has not yet been created.

## Modified and third-party distributions

Modified distributions must be presented as independently maintained and must not claim to be an
official WebGuard release unless Bashar Asaad has expressly authorized that status. Independently
maintained modified distributions must not use Bashar Asaad's personal logo, name treatment, or
visual identity in a manner that implies the distribution is official, endorsed, sponsored, or
maintained by him.

Distributors may accurately attribute their work to the original WebGuard project and may make
ordinary truthful references to Bashar Asaad as its original author and publisher. They must
preserve the copyright and permission notices required by the MIT License. A distributor may
replace the personal logo and user-facing publisher identity without changing the application's
technical interfaces.

The user-facing branding is concentrated in:

- `frontend/public/brand/`;
- `frontend/index.html` and the frontend header, footer, and professional-review component;
- the CLI heading and version output;
- the API display title; and
- the self-contained HTML report heading.

Replacing those assets and strings does not require changing the `webguard` Python import path,
the `webguard` and `webguard-api` commands, `/api/v1/` routes, or report/ruleset identifiers.
Historical technical identifiers such as the `cybernet-webguard` Python distribution and Docker
Compose project name are retained for compatibility; they do not identify CyberNet as this
release's publisher and do not confer official status.
